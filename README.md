## 🌟 About the Project

This website serves as a comprehensive digital portfolio and service hub representing **Maduranga Dahanake**. It highlights a multidisciplinary career bridging **musical arts, professional media production, software engineering, and music education**[cite: 1]. The platform is built with a responsive, modern user interface to deliver a seamless browsing experience across mobile devices, tablets, and desktops.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Sections

- **Home (`#home`):** An eye-catching landing section featuring a professional intro, dynamic headings, and direct call-to-action buttons[cite: 1].
- **About Me (`#about`):** A bilingual/localized personal summary detailing my background in violin performance, professional photography, and video editing[cite: 1].
- **What I Do (`#services`):** A grid layout outlining all core services offered, complete with dedicated navigation links to individual sub-pages[cite: 1].
- **Portraits & Apps Pages:** Dedicated galleries and tools showcasing high-resolution creative portraits and live web utilities[cite: 1].
- **Contact Hub (`#contact`):** Direct communication channels including email, physical location, and direct social messaging links[cite: 1].

## 💼 Detailed Services

- **🎻 Music & Violin:** Specializing in violin covers, instrumental tracks, live performances, and digital audio production[cite: 1].
- **📸 Photography:** Professional portrait shoots, event coverage, and creative conceptual photography[cite: 1].
- **🎬 Videography & Video Editing:** Cinematic event capture, short films, advanced color grading, dynamic transitions, and optimized social media edits[cite: 1].
- **💻 Web & App Development:** Modern UI/UX design and clean, efficient coding utilizing HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Flutter for custom digital solutions[cite: 1].
- **🛒 MD Music Shop:** A curated store offering high-quality local and international musical instruments (such as Geta Beraya, Tabla, Violins, and Guitars) along with essential accessories at reasonable prices[cite: 1].
- **🎓 MD Music Academy:** Structured professional music lessons covering the Violin, Tabla, and traditional percussion instruments through both theoretical and practical frameworks[cite: 1].
- **🛠️ My Apps & Tools:** Interactive, custom-built live web tools and applications designed to optimize music production workflows and daily tasks[cite: 1].
